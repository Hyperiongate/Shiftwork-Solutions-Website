The talk in two sentences

If you remember nothing else from the hour, remember these. They are one idea: the machine handles the part of the problem that is the same in every plant, and you handle the part that is only true in yours.

The thesis

AI does not replace the job. It replaces the part of the job nobody went to school to do.

Its counterweight

AI knows what's common. It doesn't know what's right for you.
